Granular tracking

Drill from the whole plant down to a single checkpoint.

Every record carries who, where, when and what was measured. So a number on the plant head's dashboard always opens into the exact reading that produced it.

From checkpoint to saving

How one caught reading turns into saved hours.

The saving does not come from the software. It comes from acting a few days earlier, because the reading reached the right person the minute it was taken.

Seen by the right person
